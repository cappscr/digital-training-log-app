# About Page

The primary purpose of the about page is to provide more detailed information about the Digital Training Log app.

I want the content to be broken up into a few themes

## Why the app exists

I use multiple pieces and brands of fitness hardware in my own training as such I want an app that is hardware agnostic. I find that apps like Strava have poor support for sports and training that are not well suited to GPS traces. Few, if any apps in this space, have really good user experience across mobile and desktop versions.

## Honesty about what to expect

- I am a single developer working on this as a hobby project
- Digital Training Log is not registered as a business
- I am not terribly interested in running Digital Training Log as a business, I am a professional software developer.
- In many ways the principal purpose of this app is for me to experiment with software, services, libraries etc for professional development within a software context that I actually want to use.
- I do however, want to retain the option to benefit financially from the IP of the app by selling to a competitor like Strava or TrainingPeaks even though I have no interest in competing directly with such a company at the moment
