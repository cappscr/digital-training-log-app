# Digital Training Log App

The digital training log app is a web application that is meant to be a digital analog to a paper based log for recording athletic training.

It is rooted in training for long distance running; however, will encompass other sports as well. Strength training for example, will be a first class citizen in digital training log even though it is not well suited to GPS watch metrics.

The emphasis on being an analog to a paper journal means that the app strives to be flexible in the data it allows users to input. Not purely constrained to the metrics measurable from a GPS trace.

While a spreadsheet is a dumping ground for data, this application acts as a structured system for self-reflection, allowing athletes to enforce consistency through templates that paper cannot provide.

The app bridges the gap between the chaos of a manual log and the utility of a database, automating the calculation of volume and trends—so the athlete doesn't have to—without ever prescribing behavior or offering algorithmic coaching.

The app will not even support direct integration with GPS traces to begin. Which means that the app's ethos revolve around manual data entry. The app is therefore (running watch, cycling computer, treadmill, etc.) hardware agnostic.

The app is not a social media site.

The app is meant to be athlete focused more than coach focused, although coaches could find the information within helpful and coaches are a _secondary_ audience. Athletes often need to share their training logs with coaches, this will be a supported feature.

The app will allow users to input numerical values to create summarized and calculated views (e.g. running distance and duration which allows for weekly, monthly, and yearly volume calculations); however, the app will strive to not have opinions or suggest actions based on calculations and trends.
